THE SHORT VERSION

Paint collects only the minimum amount of data needed to operate the site. We don't sell it, we don't share it with advertisers, and we don't do anything weird with it. Here's the full picture:

1. WHAT WE COLLECT

Depending on how you use Paint, we may store the following:

  • Username · chosen by you when creating an account
  • Email address · used to log in (email/password accounts only)
  • Google account info · your Google UID and email, if you sign in with Google
  • Drawings · the image data of drawings you publish, along with when they were posted
  • Upload history · how many drawings you've posted and when, to enforce posting limits

2. WHAT WE DON'T COLLECT

  • We don't track your location
  • We don't use cookies for advertising
  • We don't collect your IP address directly (Firebase may log this as part of their infrastructure, see below)
  • We don't collect any data from users who just draw without making an account

3. HOW WE USE YOUR DATA

  • To let you log in and manage your account
  • To display your drawings and username in the gallery
  • To enforce posting limits (30 drawings per day)
  • To enforce bans when rules are broken

We don't use your data for advertising, analytics, or any purpose beyond running Paint.

4. FIREBASE

Paint is built on Google Firebase, which handles authentication and stores data. Firebase may collect technical information, such as IP addresses and device information, as part of providing its services. This is governed by Google's privacy policy, which is separate from this one.

5. YOUR DATA, YOUR RIGHTS

You can:

  • Delete your drawings at any time from the gallery.
  • Change your username or email in your Account Settings.
  • Delete your account and all associated data in your Account Settings.

6. DATA RETENTION

We keep your account information and published drawings for as long as your account exists. If you delete your account, your account and its associated data will be permanently removed from Paint, although temporary backups may exist for a limited time.

7. DATA SECURITY

Your data is stored in Firebase's Realtime Database, which is protected by Firebase security rules. Passwords are handled entirely by Firebase Authentication and are never stored by us. That said, no system is 100% secure, and we can't guarantee absolute protection.

8. ACCOUNT SECURITY

You're responsible for keeping your account secure. Choose a strong password and don't share it with anyone. If you believe someone has accessed your account without permission, change your password immediately or contact us through the official Paint Discord server.

9. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

Paint uses a small number of trusted third-party services to operate, including Google Firebase for authentication and data storage. These services have their own privacy policies, which apply when you use them.

10. DEVICE BANS

If someone seriously or repeatedly violates our Terms of Service, Paint may store limited technical information necessary to enforce a ban. This information is used only for moderation and is never sold or used for advertising.

11. REPORTING CONTENT

If you find a drawing that violates our Terms of Service, you can report it using Paint's reporting feature or contact us on the official Paint Discord server. We review reports as soon as reasonably possible.

12. CHILDREN

Paint is not directed at children under 13. If you're under 13, please don't create an account. If we become aware that a child under 13 has created an account, we'll remove it.

If you're under 13, you're still welcome to use Paint to draw and download your artwork as a PNG. Please don't create an account or publish drawings to the gallery.

13. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

This policy may be updated from time to time. Any significant changes will be noted here with an updated date at the top.

14. CONTACT

Got a question or concern? Join the official Paint Discord server.
