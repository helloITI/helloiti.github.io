Reminder

Please note that your Avatars will not be automatically saved in your browser (I couldn't get that feature working...).

If you want to save your Avatars, edit them later, or share them online, click the 💾 icon, then click Save or XML in the Editor.
This will save your Avatar as a .mae file or an XML file, which you can use wherever you want.
You can also copy the Avatar link from the Editor and paste it into your browser.
This is currently the only way to have your Avatar automatically load in your browser.

(You can also share your Avatar by using the Avatar link in the Editor, no need to save a file!)

Features

  • Create customizable avatars online or from your desktop computer (more info...)
  • Save and load your avatars to and from your hard drive (more info...)
  • Supports .mii and .rcd character files as well as avatars defined in XML (more info...)
  • Export your avatars to a JPEG or PNG image for sharing on the web! (more info...)

Requires Adobe Flash Player 10 or greater.
