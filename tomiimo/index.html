Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What's this project about?
A: I wanted to test something with Tomiimo's API because I was really bored.
Anyways, it's just a simple tool that loads any user's Tomiimo's Mii head image and you can download or copy it too, see their Mii's nickname, etc.

Q: What is Tomiimo?
A: Tomiimo is a yet another Miitomo revival that is coming soon.
It brings back a lot of stuff from Miitomo which Kaerutomo didn't.
Check their website here: https://tomiimo.online

Q: How do I get my Tomiimo User ID?
A: Go to Tomiimo, click the Friends tab, "Add or invite friends", "Invite" and click "Share". Click any app (e.g Gmail), copy the friend link and visit it.
Right click or hold the "Using an older patch?" link, and copy it.
It should look like this:

(Example Link)
miitomo://friend/cb5e06b659d20c1b/000000000000
 Copy the first random text, that's your User ID.
