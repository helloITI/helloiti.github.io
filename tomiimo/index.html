Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What's this project about?
A: I wanted to test something with Tomiimo's Mii head images because I was really bored.
Anyways, it's just a simple tool that loads your Tomiimo's Mii image and you can download it too, nothing else that's very interesting.

Q: What is Tomiimo?
A: Tomiimo is yet another Miitomo revival that is coming soon.
It brings back a lot of stuff from Miitomo which Kaerutomo didn't.
Check their website here: https://tomiimo.online

Q: How do I get my Tomiimo User ID?
A: Open Tomiimo, click the Notifications button, click Account and copy your Account ID.
